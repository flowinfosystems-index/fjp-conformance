Your Flow key is on its way. Next, put Flow between your production agent and the action it can take.

1. Check your email for the Flow key

Your key begins with fjp_live_. Store it securely. It authenticates calls to the Flow judgment/control service.

2. Keep inference on your account

Third-party model usage is not included in Flow access. Your model provider, cloud account, private model, or inference gateway remains yours and bills you directly.

Your models. Your billing. Flow's judgment.

3. Put Flow before the action

curl -X POST https://resolve.flowinfo.co/api/v1/tools/judge -H "Authorization: Bearer fjp_live_..." -H "Content-Type: application/json" -d '{"context":"Proposed action: issue a $12,000 refund. Autonomous authority: $5,000."}'

Use the returned judgment and action directive to proceed, hold, or escalate before the tool executes.

4. Or connect through MCP

endpoint: https://resolve.flowinfo.co/mcp auth: Authorization: Bearer fjp_live_...

Your agent can call judge, weigh, and match as MCP tools.

5. Report outcomes

Every response includes a request_id. When the outcome becomes observable, associate it with the original judgment for later review and re-evaluation.

curl -X POST https://resolve.flowinfo.co/api/v1/outcomes -H "Authorization: Bearer fjp_live_..." -H "Content-Type: application/json" -d '{"request_id":"req_...","outcome":"confirmed"}'
